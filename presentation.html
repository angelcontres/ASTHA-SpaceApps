Proposed Solution

An offline clinical co-pilot for the crew

ASTHA spots physiological drift before symptoms appear and turns it into step-by-step actions the crew can execute.

ASTHA · NASA Space Apps 2026

One sentence: ASTHA detects drift early and turns it into action. Sense, detect, act. The key technical choice is the Modified Z-Score against each astronaut’s own baseline: it ignores outliers and individual variability, so it warns early without crying wolf.

SYNTHETIC

OSDR publishes no continuous astronaut vitals, so crew telemetry in ASTHA is synthetic, calibrated to OSD-574 ranges. Sources, thresholds and formulas come from NASA.

ASTHA · NASA Space Apps 2026

Walk the table top to bottom. Continuous heart-rate or SpO₂ streams aren’t public in OSDR, so we calibrated a synthetic 4-crew dataset against Inspiration4 and kept every threshold traceable to NASA sources.

Clinical app
